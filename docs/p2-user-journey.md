# P2 User Journey

This journey follows a Mandarin language learner who is struggling to effectively prepare for a standardized exam. During one practice exam, they realize that they have not studied several characters. Thus, to pass the exam next time, they search up what characters they need to know for the test and find a list online. While scanning through the list, they have a rough idea of which characters their textbooks have covered so far but must manually cross out each learned character while going through their book. However, along the way, they realize that some of the characters are still difficult for them, so should they still check it off or add some sort of special mark? In addition, their textbook covers characters not in this list but maybe in one for a future test. Should they also focus on future lists or just the present one? The student feels overwhelmed and decides to use _MyHanzi_ to make character tracking easier for them.

After registering, they are met with the _Home_ screen, with an initialized "Studied" collection and the option to create more to categorize character difficulty or those needed for an exam. The student is ready to add all their studied characters, so they begin going through all their textbooks and add by opening the _Search Options_ menu and either inputting the pinyin or written character. After clicking "enter", they are met with the _Characters_ screen and can scroll until they find the character they want to add. After clicking on it, it opens the _Character_ screen, which comes with a detailed description and allows the student to add it to their "Studied" collection.

When the student finishes adding all their characters, they can navigate back to the _Home_ screen, and click on whichever collection they want to focus on, opening the _Collection_ page.
